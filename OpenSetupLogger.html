What The Data Should Tell You (after ~20-30 days)

• Does the pullback actually come? Count NO / ran-away days — if it's a big share, "always pulls back" is wrong and you need a no-trade rule.
• Displacement range — is it really 100-150, or does it scale with the day's volatility? The average + spread tells you.
• Does the options level add edge? Compare Max-R when a level was there vs not. If "on a level" days aren't better, the level isn't the edge — the open is.
• Does alignment matter? Compare ALL-AGREE vs CONFLICT days. If aligned days carry the winners, that's your filter.
• Expectancy in R — the bottom line. Positive across enough days = a real setup, not a remembered one.
