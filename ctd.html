Data & sources

Live mode reads official Senate eFD + House Clerk disclosures via the Financial Modeling Prep API (/stable/senate-latest, /stable/house-latest), free tier ≈ 250 calls/day. Capitol Trades has no public browser API; its data can only be reached server-side. Senate/House Stock Watcher (once free) stopped updating in 2021.

The STOCK Act

Members must disclose securities trades over $1,000 within 45 days. Amounts are filed as ranges, never exact — volume here uses range midpoints. "Lag" = days from trade to disclosure; a red flag marks filings past 45 days.

Alerts & limits

Alerts fire while this tab is open — the page polls the live feed every 5 min. Background alerts (tab closed) need a push server, which this serverless build deliberately avoids. Nothing here is investment advice.
